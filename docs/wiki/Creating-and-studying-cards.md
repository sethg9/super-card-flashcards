# Creating and studying cards

Create a deck, name it, and select **Add card**. Enter a question on the front and an answer on the back. Check the live previews, then choose **Save card**. Completed edits also autosave. Search cards by their content or tags; use each card's edit control to revise it.

Choose **Study deck** to study a deck, or a card's play button to start there. Every card remains available: there are no due dates or daily limits. **Shuffle** changes the session order; **Restart** begins that order again. Moving to another card resets it to the front.

## Keyboard controls

| Key | While studying |
| --- | --- |
| Click the card, Space, Up, or Down | Flip the current card |
| Left / Right in Flashcards | Previous / next card |
| Left / Right in Learn | Still learning / Know it |
| Enter on the focused card | Flip |
| Page Up / Page Down in the card content | Scroll a long card |
| Escape | Return to the deck |

Study shortcuts leave typing, editable fields, dropdowns, other controls, and open dialogs alone. Holding a key does not repeatedly flip. The **About & storage** guide also lists the controls and your data location.

For math and pictures, see [MathJax and images](https://github.com/sethg9/super-card-flashcards/wiki/MathJax-and-images). Animation controls are in [Appearance](https://github.com/sethg9/super-card-flashcards/wiki/Themes-and-appearance).

## Learn mode

Select **Learn deck** to begin with every card. Flip before or after answering, then choose **Still learning** (X or Left arrow) or **Know it** (checkmark or Right arrow). A brief pile indicator confirms the choice; rapid repeated inputs are ignored while the current card is classified. The next card starts on its front. Reduced motion removes the movement.

At the end of a round, review the totals and choose **Review remaining cards**. Only cards marked Still learning appear in the next round. Repeat until none remain, then study the full deck again or return to the library. If every card needs more practice, the next round includes them all. **Exit Learn** or Escape leaves the session. Progress is temporary: it never changes your deck, hides cards permanently, or creates a schedule.
